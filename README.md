# Bulk SMS Nigeria

This repository provides information about Bulk SMS services, SMS communication solutions, and business messaging platforms for organizations across Nigeria. It is intended for businesses looking to improve customer communication, automate notifications, run promotional campaigns, and connect with customers through fast and reliable SMS technology.

---

## About Bulk SMS

Bulk SMS enables businesses to send large volumes of text messages to customers, employees, and business contacts efficiently. It is commonly used for promotional campaigns, OTP verification, transactional notifications, payment alerts, appointment reminders, delivery updates, and business announcements.

A reliable Bulk SMS platform can help businesses streamline communication, improve customer engagement, and support scalable messaging requirements.
